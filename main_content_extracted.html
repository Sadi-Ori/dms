Muslims Around the World FAQs

MATW Project, short for Muslims Around The World, is an international Islamic charity founded by Ali Banat in 2016. We deliver food, water, medical aid, orphan care and emergency relief across more than 30 countries, funded by Zakat, Sadaqah and Sadaqah Jariyah from Muslims around the world.

When you give to a specific project, 100% of your donation, after merchant and banking fees, goes to that project. That covers direct project costs and the fundraising that brings more money back into the same cause. Administration is not deducted from your gift.

Yes. MATW distributes Zakat only to recipients eligible under Shariah, in line with the eight categories set out in the Quran. Select Zakat as your donation type at checkout.

Sadaqah Jariyah is continuous charity that keeps earning reward after you have passed away. At MATW it applies to water wells, masjid construction, orphan and widow sponsorship, education and housing.

MATW works in more than 30 countries, with major programmes in Palestine, Lebanon, Yemen, Pakistan, Bangladesh and across West Africa, including Togo, where Ali Banat first began the charity work in 2016.
